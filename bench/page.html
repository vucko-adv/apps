Pixels are the app's own: image layers are its raw RGBA pushes, gauges run its real GLSL shader in WebGL. Brightness, color and blur on the real display will differ; only the car shows that.

Center screen

status: waiting for the app
The app's page on the car's center screen: the status row, then the settings. A change here is pressed on the kit's stand-in HMI, the same way the car's drop-downs and checkboxes send it, and saved in bench/.data/<app id>/settings.json. A layout file saved into bench/.data/<app id>/layouts/ overrides the built-in one of that name, and the app reloads it by itself.

Vehicle signals

Demo drive is the kit's simulator (sim.js). It has no boost signal, so boost reads "--" here, as on a car that does not fill that field.

Layers

conn:refkindclass/zrectcar
conn: the app's connection to cluster-render (1 is its own; a layout's "conn" widgets open more, each with its own 8 layers). Red: over the layer cap, state waiting, not drawn. The car column flags a layer the driver can't fully see (hood, BMW's gear box or belt light, outside the HUD window); hover for the reason.
